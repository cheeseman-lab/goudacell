How to label

  1. Look at the tile. Magenta lines are a model's cell outlines, yellow lines are nuclei (for orientation only). The map at the top left shows where the tile sits in the whole image; the scale bar is in µm.
  2. Fix only what is wrong. Not a cell or junk: click it, press Delete. One cell cut into pieces: Merge. Two cells in one outline: Split. A missed cell: Draw or Brush. Outline too big: Erase.
  3. One cell = one outline, however many nuclei it has. Tag such cells Multinucleated.
  4. Accept the tile c when every cell has exactly one correct outline, then go to the next n.
  5. Send the labels back: press Export labels (top right) and send the .zip file. Your work is also kept in this browser, so you can stop and continue later.

All tools and keys

Select v click an object. Merge m click the pieces of one object in turn. Split s drag a line through an object that holds two. Draw w click around a missing object, then double-click or press Enter. Brush b paints the selected object, or a new one if nothing is selected. Erase e removes pixels. Fill f fills an enclosed gap. Brush and fill never paint over a neighbour unless you hold Shift. Delete removes the selected object. If the other model's outlines look closer for a tile, switch to them first with Start from. The Display controls change only the view, never the labels.

Tags: 0 clears. Other keys: z undo, n/p next or previous tile, hold h to hide outlines, [ ] brush size.
